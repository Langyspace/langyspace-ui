## Exact copy

### B1/B2

**Introduce yourself**

_Get to know your teacher. Share a little about yourself and ask back_

1. **Teacher:** Hi, I’m [first name]. Nice to meet you! Tell me a little about yourself
   **Learner cue:** Introduce yourself, share one detail and ask your teacher about herself
   **Tip:** Try “I’m from…” or “I’m based in…”. Add one detail, then ask “How about you?”
   **Optional model:** Hi, I’m … I’m from …, and I’m currently … How about you?

2. **Teacher:** What do you enjoy doing in your free time?
   **Learner cue:** Talk about one interest and ask your teacher what she enjoys
   **Tip:** Try “I enjoy…” or “I’m really into…”. React to your teacher’s answer and ask a follow-up
   **Optional model:** I’m really into … at the moment. How about you — what do you enjoy doing?

3. **Teacher:** What would you like to feel more confident doing in English?
   **Learner cue:** Share one goal and ask your teacher for her perspective
   **Tip:** Try “I’d like to get better at…” or “I’d love to feel more comfortable…”
   **Optional model:** I’d like to feel more comfortable … What would you suggest I focus on?

### C1/C2

**Introduce yourself**

_Have a relaxed chat with your teacher. Share a little about yourself and get to know each other_

1. **Teacher:** Hi, I’m [first name]. Good to meet you! How’s your day going?
   **Learner cue:** Introduce yourself, share a small detail about your day and ask back
   **Tip:** “Pretty good, thanks” / “Not bad” / “It’s been a bit of a day”. Add one real detail, then ask back
   **Optional model:** Hi, I’m … Not bad, thanks — I’ve just … How’s your day been?

2. **Teacher:** So, what keeps you busy these days?
   **Learner cue:** Share a little about your life at the moment and keep the chat going
   **Tip:** “I’m based in…” / “Lately, I’ve been…”. One or two details are enough; use “How about you?”
   **Optional model:** I’m based in …, and lately I’ve been … That’s been keeping me busy. How about you?

3. **Teacher:** And when you get a bit of time to yourself, what do you get up to?
   **Learner cue:** Talk about an interest and invite your teacher to share hers
   **Tip:** “I’ve been getting into…” / “I’m a bit of a…”. Pick what sounds like you; ask a genuine follow-up
   **Optional model:** I’ve been getting into … lately. How about you — anything you’ve been enjoying?
