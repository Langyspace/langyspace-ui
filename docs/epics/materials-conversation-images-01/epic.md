# Recorded dialogue controls and refreshed lesson imagery

Teachers and students can explicitly play the recorded conversations in the A1, B1 and B2 trial
lessons, A2.01 and the two B2.01 dialogue pages. The action uses the existing brand/md Button and
the shared recorded-audio controller. The selected image cohort replaces the approved illustration
family, including four shared portraits. B1.01 has a separate catalog cover composed for 16:10.

The adoption contains 87 source/media/catalog paths: 11 source/test/story files, 39 existing
recording copies, 35 image replacements, one additional cover and one catalog field. It preserves
lesson content, existing audio, public exports, dependencies and the shared theme contract.

See [requirements](requirements.md), [technical plan](technical-plan.md), [tasks](tasks.md),
[progress and comparison matrix](progress.md), [source receipt](adoption-receipt.json) and
[media provenance](media-provenance.json). Current delivery stops at a locally validated,
uncommitted source checkpoint. Official exports, Claude originals, Lab, Teacher and staging need
their subsequent scoped integration steps.
