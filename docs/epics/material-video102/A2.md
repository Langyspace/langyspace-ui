# Vídeos — A2

Prévia local; a professora controla o player oficial. A aluna observa e responde oralmente. Quatro perguntas com dica opcional e resposta completa.

## A2.01 · What is he doing now?

**Na tela:** Watch Emir use the machine, then compare a usual habit with today

**Pratica:** Present continuous in a visible task

**Trecho:** [British Council | English · Giving instructions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LLvGYEjutWo) · 1:30–2:10

[Abrir local](http://127.0.0.1:8955/?page=s_video102#a2-01)

1. **What is Emir learning to use?**  
   Dica: Look at the machine and the drink  
   Resposta: He’s learning to use a coffee machine

2. **Who is helping him?**  
   Dica: Listen to the person giving instructions  
   Resposta: Vanya is helping him

3. **What is he doing before he presses the final button?**  
   Dica: Follow the instructions about connecting it  
   Resposta: He’s connecting the coffee holder to the machine

4. **What do you usually drink, and what are you drinking today?**  
   Dica: Contrast usually with today  
   Resposta: Example: I usually drink coffee, but today I’m drinking tea
