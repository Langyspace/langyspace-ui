# Vídeos — B1

Prévia local; a professora controla o player oficial. A aluna observa e responde oralmente. Quatro perguntas com dica opcional e resposta completa.

## B1.01 · Time spent or work finished?

**Na tela:** Watch the picture round, then explain the difference using your own week

**Pratica:** Present perfect progress versus completed results

**Trecho:** [BBC Learning English · The Grammar Gameshow — present perfect simple and continuous](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lvoc5vDrb8E) · 2:39–3:25

[Abrir local](http://127.0.0.1:8955/?page=s_video102#b1-01)

1. **What has the person been doing?**  
   Dica: Listen to the first sentence about the picture  
   Resposta: He’s been writing letters

2. **Which sentence focuses on completed letters?**  
   Dica: Listen for written rather than been writing  
   Resposta: “He’s written a number of letters” focuses on completed results

3. **Which question fits the continuous example: how many or how long?**  
   Dica: Listen to Leslie’s contrast at the end  
   Resposta: How long fits the continuous example

4. **What have you been working on, and what have you finished?**  
   Dica: Give one ongoing activity and one completed result  
   Resposta: Example: I’ve been studying English this week, and I’ve finished two lessons

## B1.04 · A clue is not proof

[Abrir local](http://127.0.0.1:8955/?page=s_video102#b1-04)

   Dica: Look for the detail about his hair  
   Resposta: Phil’s hair is wet

2. **Which word presents rain as an almost certain explanation?**  
   Dica: Listen to the first example

## B1.12 · Office, home or a mix?

**Na tela:** Watch the case for working away from an office, then negotiate a balanced routine

**Pratica:** Preferences, drawbacks and a workable compromise

**Trecho:** [TED · Matt Mullenweg — Why working from home is good for business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x6fIseKzzH0) · 1:35–2:07

[Abrir local](http://127.0.0.1:8955/?page=s_video102#b1-12)

1. **What kind of freedom does he want workers to have?**  
   Dica: Listen for autonomy  
   Resposta: He wants them to have autonomy over how they work

2. **What does he say workers can gain by not commuting?**  
   Dica: Listen to how the saved time can be used  
   Resposta: They can use the time for things that matter to them

3. **Does he present this as equally suitable for every kind of company?**  
   Dica: Listen to the technology-company comment and the next question  
   Resposta: No. He calls it ideal for a technology company and raises the question of other workplaces

4. **Negotiate a work routine with one benefit and one drawback**  
   Dica: Acknowledge a limitation before proposing a compromise  
   Resposta: Example: Home is quieter, but I miss quick team discussions. How about two office days?
