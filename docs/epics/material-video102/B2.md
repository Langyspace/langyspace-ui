**Na tela:** Watch the mountain story, then retell the background and turning point

**Pratica:** Narrative tenses with a clear sequence

   Dica: Listen for the height and the landform  
   Resposta: The traveller was trying to cross a mountain pass almost four thousand metres high

2. **Why was progress already difficult before the storm?**  
   Dica: Listen for the air and the equipment  
   Resposta: The air was thin and the traveller was carrying a heavy pack

3. **What changed just as the traveller got near the top?**  
   Dica: Listen to the interruption in the story  
   Resposta: A storm arrived, with lightning all around

4. **Retell the lead-up and turning point in two or three sentences**  
   Dica: Use background, an earlier action and the sudden event  
   Resposta: Example: The traveller had been climbing with a heavy pack. As they approached the top, a storm hit
