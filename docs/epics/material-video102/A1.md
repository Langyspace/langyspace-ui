# Vídeos — A1

Prévia local; a professora controla o player oficial. A aluna observa e responde oralmente. Quatro perguntas com dica opcional e resposta completa.

## A1.01 · Meet Vanya

**Na tela:** Watch the introductions, then answer the questions

**Pratica:** Introductions, context and a personal reply

**Trecho:** [British Council | English · Meeting new people](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HooNbcbgi9U) · 0:59–1:33

[Abrir local](http://127.0.0.1:8955/?page=s_video_intro#a1-01)

1. **What’s the man’s name?**  
   Dica: Listen for “I’m…” after Vanya asks about Paul  
   Resposta: His name is Emir

2. **Where are they?**  
   Dica: Look at the room and listen for the place Emir names  
   Resposta: They’re at an office

3. **What does Yuna say to welcome Vanya?**  
   Dica: Listen to Yuna’s last words  
   Resposta: She says “Welcome to the team”

4. **You meet Vanya. How do you introduce yourself?**  
   Dica: Start with Hi, then say your name  
   Resposta: Example: Hi, Vanya! I’m [your name]. Nice to meet you
